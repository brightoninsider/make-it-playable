# Creative brief and source ledger

Use this for the first proposal, factual work, branded work, supplied material, or any game using a real person, event, place, organisation, product, or story.

## Brief that is small enough to use

Write these decisions before expanding production:

| Decision | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and audience | Why this game exists and who should want another round. |
| First-minute promise | The feeling, skill, or insight a new player should get quickly. |
| Core loop | Verb → visible consequence → risk/choice → progress → success or retry. |
| Device promise | Phone orientation/input and desktop support actually being built. |
| Art direction | Camera, scale, palette, density, motion, and what looks intentionally finished. |
| Constraints | Time, runtime, rights, accessibility, hosting, and no-go choices. |

State whether the work is personal/unbranded or uses a supplied brand. A name, logo, palette, voice, and assets are optional inputs. Keep the game’s own objective clearer than any brand message.

## Source ledger: only where reality is being represented

Do not invent a ledger for a fictional kitchen game or abstract arcade piece. When facts or outside assets matter, use this compact table:

| Item | Supported fact / source | Creative translation | Rights / consent | Open question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <item> | URL, supplied file, or interview/date | What play changes or invents | licence, permission, attribution | what must be checked |

Possession of a file is not publication permission. Keep rights receipts and supplied originals outside the browser runtime. A generated image can establish a visual direction; it does not establish factual accuracy, consent, or a right to reuse a reference.

## First human review

Before costly art, show a representative gameplay frame next to the playable tracer. Ask separately:

1. Does this look like the intended game?
2. Does the first loop feel worth replaying?
3. Which specific mechanic, camera, art, UI, or sound issue should change next?

Record the answer as an acceptance delta with observable evidence. Do not turn silence or a screenshot approval into confirmation that the game is fun.
